\# SmartFarmAIERP Blueprint



\## Vision



SmartFarmAIERP is an AI-powered ERP platform designed for farms across Tanzania and East Africa.



Initial focus:

\- Poultry farms



Future expansion:

\- Crop farming

\- Dairy

\- Fish farming

\- Mixed farming





\## Core Modules



1\. Core Platform

2\. Farm Management

3\. Poultry Management

4\. Inventory Engine

5\. Procurement

6\. Sales

7\. Finance

8\. Human Resources

9\. AI Intelligence

10\. Reporting \& Analytics
